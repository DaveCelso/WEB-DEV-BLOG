Oslob Whale Sharks

A beautiful waterfall known for its clear blue water.

A historical landmark placed by Ferdinand Magellan in 1521.

Tops Lookout

Temple of Leah

A grand Roman-style temple built as a symbol of love.

Sirao Flower Garden

Known as the Little Amsterdam of Cebu because of its flowers.

Simala Shrine

A famous pilgrimage site visited by many devotees.

Malapascua Island

A small island known for diving and thresher sharks.
